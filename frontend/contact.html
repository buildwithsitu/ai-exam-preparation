Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Answers to common questions about ExamMate AI:

How do I change my target learning path or career goal?

Navigate to your Profile & Settings page. Under the “Target Career Goal” section, choose your new track (e.g. Software Development, Data Analytics, or Full-Stack Web) and click “Update Target Track”. Your dashboard roadmap and recommendations will immediately adapt.

Is ExamMate AI completely free for students?

Yes. All learning paths, study resources, question banks, and timed mock tests on ExamMate AI are open and 100% free for students. We believe high-quality education should be accessible without paywalls.

How are question derivations and answers verified?

All questions are peer-reviewed against official syllabus blueprints and standard academic references. If you spot an error, simply use the form above to submit an erratum report, and our academic desk will verify and update it.
